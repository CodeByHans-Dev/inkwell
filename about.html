About Inkwell

Inkwell is a storybook website in manga style. It has 30 original one-chapter stories across 10 genres, from action and romance to horror and comedy.

Use the library to search or filter by genre, tap Show synopsis for a quick summary, open Details for a popup, and like your favorites. Ink mode switches the page to a dark theme, and a progress bar tracks how far you have read.
