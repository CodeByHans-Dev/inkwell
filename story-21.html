Rooftop Club Rules

by Chika Inoue

Synopsis

Four misfit students make a club out of eating lunch on the school roof.

Chapter 1: Four Seats

Rule one of the Rooftop Club is that there are no rules. Rule two is that everyone brings a snack to share. Nobody remembers who wrote them.

Today a new girl climbs the stairs with a bento and a nervous smile. The four of them look at each other, scoot over, and make room on the bench.
